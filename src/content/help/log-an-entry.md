Logging an **entry** records a score for a tracking type at a point in time.
This is the core, everyday action in the app.

## Before you start

- You need at least one tracking type. See
  [Define a tracking type](/help/define-a-tracking-type).

## Steps

1. Tap **New entry** — the **+** in the bottom bar, or **New entry** on the Home
   screen.
2. Pick a tracking type from the chips at the top (each is tagged **1D** or
   **2D**).
3. Set your score:
   - For a **1D** type, drag the slider.
   - For a **2D** type, drag the dot on the pad to set both values at once.
4. *(Optional)* Change the **Date**, add a **Title**, **Notes**, or **Tags**.
5. Tap **Save entry**.

## What you'll see

A confirmation appears and you return to the Home screen, where your entry shows
up under **Recent**. Its values feed the **Quick insight** cards and the
**Charts** tab.
