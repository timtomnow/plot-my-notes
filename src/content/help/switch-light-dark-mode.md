Plot My Notes can follow your device's appearance setting or stick to a light or
dark theme you choose.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and find the **Appearance** section.
2. Under **Theme**, tap **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

## What you'll see

The app switches to the chosen theme immediately. **System** follows your
device's light/dark setting and updates automatically when that changes.
