You can change any entry after logging it — adjust its score, date, notes, or
tags — or delete it entirely if it was a mistake.

## Before you start

- You need at least one logged entry. See [Log an entry](/help/log-an-entry).

## Steps

1. Open the **Entries** tab.
2. Tap the entry you want to change. Its details open.
3. To change it, tap **Edit**, update the values, and tap **Save changes**.
4. To remove it, tap **Delete** and confirm.

## What you'll see

After editing, a confirmation appears and the entry shows its updated values in
the list and on charts. After deleting, the entry is removed everywhere.
