All your axes, tracking types, and entries live locally in your browser. Export
a backup file before clearing site data, switching devices, or experimenting —
and import it later to bring that data back.

## Before you start

- Exporting is always safe. **Import → Replace** wipes your current data, so
  export a fresh backup first if you're unsure.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** and find the **Data** section.
2. To save a backup, tap **Export**. A JSON file downloads to your device.
3. To bring data in, tap **Import…** and choose a backup file.
4. Review what the file contains, then choose how to apply it:
   - **Merge** — add anything that isn't already there, keeping your current
     data.
   - **Replace** — wipe current data, then load the file. This can't be undone.
5. *(Optional)* Tap **Restore from ttn-backup** to pull from the separate
   ttn-backup utility, which snapshots all your TTN apps together.

## What you'll see

After export, a confirmation shows the backup downloaded. After import, a
summary reports how many axes, types, and entries were merged or replaced; a
replace reloads the app fresh.
