The **Charts** tab turns your logged entries into a picture. A 1D type shows a
line over time; a 2D type shows a scatter plot of the value pairs plus a line
for each axis. Charts are read-only — you build them just by choosing what to
show.

## Before you start

- Log a few entries first so there's something to plot. See
  [Log an entry](/help/log-an-entry).

## Steps

1. Open the **Charts** tab.
2. Tap a tracking-type chip to choose what to chart.
3. Pick a time range: **7d**, **30d**, or **All**.
4. *(2D types)* Turn on **Connect by date** to join the scatter points in time
   order.
5. Tap any point to open that entry's details.

## What you'll see

For a 1D type, a line chart with a dashed 7-point moving average. For a 2D type,
a scatter plot — brighter dots are more recent — above a line chart for each
axis. Any [bands](/help/add-bands-to-an-axis) or
[regions](/help/add-regions-to-a-2d-type) you defined are shaded behind the
data. A count of entries in range is shown below.
