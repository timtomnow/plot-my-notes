An **axis** is one measurable dimension of how you feel, such as *Happiness*,
*Energy*, or *Focus*. Every tracking type is built from one or two axes, so
creating an axis is usually the first step when you want to track something new.

## Before you start

- Decide what the axis measures and its range (for example, 0–10).

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Axes**.
2. Tap **New axis** (or **Create first axis** if you have none yet).
3. Enter a **Name**, e.g. `Happiness`.
4. Optionally add a **Short description** (shown under the name) and a **Full
   description** (shown behind an info button).
5. Set the **Min**, **Max**, and **Step** for the scale. A 0–10 scale with a
   step of 1 is a good default — the step must divide the range evenly.
6. *(Optional)* Add **bands** to colour regions of the scale. See
   [Add coloured bands to an axis](/help/add-bands-to-an-axis).
7. Tap **Create**.

## What you'll see

Your new axis appears in the Axes list with its range and step. It's now
available to pick when you
[define a tracking type](/help/define-a-tracking-type), which is what you'll
actually log day to day.
