The **Home** screen gives you a glance at the last seven days without opening a
full chart. It shows a **Quick insight** card for each tracking type you've
logged, plus your most recent entries.

## Before you start

- Log a few entries so the cards have data. See
  [Log an entry](/help/log-an-entry).

## Steps

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Read each **Quick insight** card: it shows the 7-day average for the type's
   axis (or both axes for a 2D type) and an arrow showing whether it's trending
   up, down, or holding steady.
3. Tap a card to jump to its full chart.
4. Under **Recent**, tap **See all →** to open the full Entries list.

## What you'll see

Cards show the current 7-day average with a coloured up/down arrow and the
change amount; a flat dash means little movement. Types you haven't logged yet
don't appear here.
