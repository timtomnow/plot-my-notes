Plot My Notes lets you track how you feel over time. You define **axes** (a
scale like *Happiness* or *Energy*), combine one or two of them into a
**tracking type**, and then log **entries** against it. This guide walks the
three setup steps in order, the first time you open the app.

## Before you start

- Nothing to install or sign in to — everything is stored locally on your
  device.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Axes** and create at least one axis (for example, a 0–10
   *Energy* scale).
2. Open **Settings → Tracking Types** and create a type built from your axis.
   Pick one axis for a simple **1D** type, or turn on **2D tracking** to combine
   two.
3. Tap **New entry** (the **+** in the bottom bar, or **New entry** on the Home
   screen).
4. Choose your tracking type, set the score, and tap **Save entry**.

## What you'll see

Your entry appears on the **Home** screen under **Recent**, and a **Quick
insight** card starts tracking its 7-day average. Once you've logged a few
entries, the **Charts** tab fills in.
